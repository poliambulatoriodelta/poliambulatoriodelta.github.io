Our philosophy

Our design philosophy stems from the desire to move beyond the classic clinical setting, creating a space that feels more homely, welcoming and reassuring. The palette, inspired by the colours of the earth — clay, terracotta and sage — blends with natural materials such as oak and glass, balanced to combine warmth, lightness and brightness. The aim is to convey a feeling of calm and relaxation, encouraging a more relaxed approach to the appointment and an environment that feels familiar and close to the person.

Parking just steps away

Just 40 metres from the centre, on Viale Marco Polo, you'll find convenient private parking: simply take a short internal staircase to reach us easily.

Cookie Policy

Notice on the cookies and tracking tools used by this website.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that the websites you visit send to your browser, where they are stored and then sent back to the same websites on your next visit. They can be "technical", necessary for the website to work properly, or "profiling", used to track browsing and offer targeted advertising.

2. The cookies used by this website

This website does not install any profiling cookies for advertising purposes. However, only with your consent, it uses Google Analytics for statistical purposes, to understand how the website is used and to improve its content.

TypeProviderPurpose
Browser local storage (localStorage)Poliambulatorio Delta (own website)Remembers the choice made in the cookie banner, so as not to ask for it again on every visit. It is not a cookie in the technical sense and is not shared with third parties.
Statistical cookies (activated only with consent)Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited)Collects information in aggregate form on the use of the website (pages visited, traffic sources, duration of visit) for statistical purposes. It is activated only if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner.
Third-party cookies (activated only with consent)Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited)Needed to show the map on the Contact page. They are installed only if you choose "Accept all" in the banner, or by clicking to view the map directly on the Contact page.
Connection to external fontsGoogle Fonts (Google Ireland Limited)Loading of the website's typefaces. It involves a technical connection to Google's servers (with transmission of the IP address) but does not install cookies.

If you choose "Necessary only" in the cookie banner, Google Analytics is not activated and no browsing data are sent to Google.

3. Links to WhatsApp

The "Book on WhatsApp" buttons and the "Request information" form open the WhatsApp app or website in a separate page/tab. The Poliambulatorio Delta website does not install any cookies of its own linked to this function; any use of cookies by WhatsApp takes place according to its own policy, external to this website.

Manage your preferences

You can review or change your choice regarding statistical cookies (Google Analytics) and third-party cookies (Google Maps) at any time using the button below.

4. How to disable cookies in your browser

As well as managing your preferences directly on this website, you can at any time delete the cookies already saved and block new ones through your browser settings. The procedures vary depending on the browser used (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge): you can consult your browser's help pages for specific instructions.
